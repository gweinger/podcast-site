gweinger.com · colour study

Six earth-tone palettes for the Introvert Army

Each card applies one palette to the same slice of the site: nav, hero with your headshot, main action (currently yellow), secondary link button (currently bright blue), light band and footer. Click a card to see that palette on a full home page; use the arrow keys to step through them. Every palette comes from a published source: Sanzo Wada's colour combinations, the US Army brand guide, or the traditional Japanese colour tables. The dashed card is today's navy scheme, shown for comparison.

Sources

Sanzo Wada, A Dictionary of Color Combinations (1933–34, reissued by Seigensha 2010). Hex values from the mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations dataset; combination numbers match the book.

U.S. Army Brand Guidelines v1.0, March 2023, colour specification pages.

Traditional Japanese colours (dentō-shoku): Wikipedia, Traditional colors of Japan; names cross-checked against nipponcolors.com.

The full-screen view follows the layout of the current gweinger.com home page. The glow behind the headshot uses a light colour from each palette, the same way the live site uses a blue glow to separate the navy sweater from the navy hero.

Tints marked "derived" are not in the source; they are pale versions of a source colour for page backgrounds.
